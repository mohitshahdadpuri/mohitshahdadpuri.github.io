If your phone has a Google Play Games profile, the game signs in with it by itself and creates a player account for you automatically. Your progress is then uploaded to our database without any further step from you. You can stop this, or delete everything, as the table shows.

3. What we process

A. Player accounts and cloud save

When the game signs you in with Play Games, or you choose Google sign-in, it uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

Firebase Authentication keeps your account:

  • an account ID that Firebase creates;
  • how you signed in (Play Games or Google), and your Play Games player ID or Google account ID;
  • your name: your Play Games gamer tag, or your Google account name;
  • if you sign in with Google, your email address and a link to your profile picture (from Google; the game doesn't use them);
  • when the account was created and last signed in, and the IP address and device details used to sign in (Firebase keeps these for security and abuse prevention).

Cloud Firestore keeps your player record:

  • your name and how you signed in;
  • when you first and last played, how many days you've played, and the game version and language;
  • your progress: levels cleared, stars, best scores and times, Daily and Weekly puzzles solved, Free Play solves, streak, shards, achievements and leaderboard scores;
  • a backup of your saved game (for a Google account, this is your cloud save);
  • your device's push message token (section 3E).

We use it to create and manage your account, to back up and restore your progress, to provide the game's features, and to see how players progress so we can improve the game. We don't sell it or use it for advertising. We keep it until you delete your account. Player records are kept in Google's Mumbai, India data centre (asia-south1).

B. Google Play Games

If you're signed in to Google Play Games on your phone, the game signs you in when it opens; otherwise you can sign in at any time from Settings → Play Games by tapping SIGN IN. Google Play Games itself stays signed in on your phone: it is your Google account, managed in Android Settings, and the game can't sign you out of it at the Android OS level.

While you're signed in, the game saves your progress (such as levels, stars, streak and shards) to your Google account through Play Games Saved Games. Google's Play Games software on your phone sends Google your Play Games player ID and gamer tag, the save, and its own analytics and diagnostics. Signing in with Play Games also gives our Firebase account your player ID and gamer tag (section 3A). You can also link your Google Account directly (ADD GOOGLE) to keep your account accessible across multiple sign-in methods. Google keeps the Play Games data under Google's Privacy Policy.

  • Achievements and leaderboards: while you're signed in, the game tells Play Games which achievements you've earned, your total stars and your longest streak. Leaderboards show your Play Games name and score to other players, as your Play Games profile settings allow.
  • To stop it: tap SIGN OUT in Settings → Account (which switches the game to Guest mode and pauses automatic cloud sync), or in Android Settings → Google → All services → Play Games → Sign-in account → "Change the account by game", choose Sign out for KERNOS: Offline Logic Puzzles. (Settings → Play Games in the game has a button that opens it.) While the game is signed out, it saves on your phone only and sends Play Games nothing: no cloud save, achievements or scores. It also sends nothing to your player record. You can re-enable Play Games sync whenever you want by tapping SIGN IN in Settings → Play Games.
  • To delete it: use DELETE ACCOUNT in Settings → Account (which deletes this game's Play Games Saved Games snapshot if linked), or delete your Play Games data for this game or your Play Games profile in the Play Games app or in your Google Account settings.

C. Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics records events as you play: levels started and finished (with stars, hints used and time taken), Daily, Weekly and Free Play solves, sign-ins, when the app is opened and updated, and whether you saw or tapped the update prompt, with the version number it offered.

With these events, Google's Analytics software on your phone sends Google an app-instance ID, device and app details (model, Android version, language, game version) and your approximate location (country and region, from your IP address). Firebase Analytics is configured so it does not collect your device's advertising ID. We don't send your name, email or account ID with them. That doesn't necessarily make the data anonymous: identifiers and device details can still be personal data under some laws.

We use Analytics to see how the game is played and to balance the puzzles. Analytics runs in every mode, including as a guest. Google keeps event data for 2 months (the retention set in our Analytics settings) and treats it as described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

D. Advertising removed

Version 3.0.3 and later do not serve advertisements, request advertising IDs, or include Google Mobile Ads or the UMP consent SDK. There is no ad consent or Privacy options entry in these versions. Firebase Analytics remains enabled as described in section 3C.

Older installed versions may still request ads through Google AdMob until they are updated or the advertising service is retired. Those versions may send Google advertising IDs, approximate location derived from IP addresses, ad interactions and diagnostics, and offer Google's consent controls where required. Update the game to use the ad-free version.

E. Push messages and game configuration

So we can send you a game message, Firebase gives your phone a token (and an installation ID), which we keep in your player record if you have an account. Messages appear on the "Messages from KERNOS" notification channel. Turn them off in Android's notification settings; that doesn't delete your account.

Firebase Remote Config also uses the Firebase installation ID and basic app and device details (game version, OS version, language, country and time zone) to fetch live game balance and store configuration parameters.

F. Game updates

The game can ask Google Play whether a newer version is available and, if you tap UPDATE, have Google Play download and install it. Google Play does this on your phone under Google's terms. We receive nothing from it; the only record on our side is the Analytics event described above. Nothing is downloaded or installed unless you tap UPDATE.

G. What stays on your phone

The game keeps your progress, stars, streak, hint charges, settings and a record of the steps you took this session (used to build a bug report) in the app's private storage on your phone. The steps and settings aren't sent to us. Your progress is backed up to your player record only if you have an account (section 3A).

Android may include your game progress in your own device backup to your Google account, if you have backup turned on. That backup is between you and Google, and we can't read it.

Reminders (streak notifications) are scheduled and shown on your phone; nothing about them is sent to a server. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in Android's settings.

H. Bug reports and feedback you choose to email us

Send bug report. If you choose Send bug report (in Pause, Settings or the Home menu, or after the game closes unexpectedly), the game prepares a report and opens your email app with it attached, addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. A report contains:

  • your device model and Android version;
  • your screen, language, time zone and battery-saver settings;
  • the game version;
  • your game settings and progress;
  • the steps you took in the game since you opened it;
  • the error details if the game closed unexpectedly;
  • what you typed;
  • if you choose, a screenshot of the game.

Send feedback. If you choose Send feedback, your email app opens with a 1–5 rating, what you typed, the game version, a short summary of your progress, and your device model, Android version and screen settings. As with reports, nothing is sent unless you send the email.

Reports and feedback never contain an account, contacts, location or advertising identifiers. Like any email, your message reaches us with your email address as the sender. Please don't put passwords, payment details or other people's personal information in them. We use them only to find and fix bugs and to improve the game, and we don't use them for advertising.

I. Purchases

The game offers three items through Google Play, subject to regional availability: Starter Protocol (starter_supplies: a one-time pack of 20 hints and 500 shards), a Hint Booster (20 hints) and a Shard Pack (500 shards). On your phone, the game queries Google Play Billing for your Play Store country code (processed locally in memory and not stored on our servers) to determine regional purchase availability. Google Play takes the payment and tells the game that it was made. We never receive your card, UPI or other payment details.

  • What we receive from Google Play: a purchase token, the product, the quantity, the time and the order number. Our server (a Google Cloud Function) checks the token with Google, so a purchase can't be faked, and then confirms it to Google Play.
  • What we store, linked to your player account in Cloud Firestore (Mumbai, India): which products you bought and the totals of hints and shards you bought (your entitlements), and one record per purchase with its token's digest, product, quantity, time and order number. You need a player account to buy, because these records are kept against it; a guest is asked to sign in first.
  • Historical purchases: remove_ads and the original starter_protocol remain recognized for purchase history and restore. They are no longer sold in this version. The former ad-free entitlement has no gameplay effect; the original Starter pack keeps its original hint and shard grants.
  • When a payment is pending (for example UPI), nothing is added until Google Play confirms it.
  • Refunds are decided by Google Play. If a purchase is refunded, reversed or charged back, the unused hints and shards from that purchase are deducted from your balance (floored at zero; any part already used is forgiven).

Your player record and account are kept in Google Firebase's data centre in Mumbai, India (asia-south1). Google's own services (Analytics, Play Games, Google Play; AdMob in older versions) process data in Google's data centres around the world. The server code that checks purchases runs in Mumbai; the code that removes purchase records after an account is deleted runs in Google Cloud in the United States (Iowa) and only handles the account's ID, product and order details.

If you live in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, your data reaches India and Google's other locations. Where the law requires safeguards for a transfer between countries, we rely on the safeguards in Google's terms for its services, including the Standard Contractual Clauses in Firebase's data processing terms.

9. Deleting your account and data

In the game:

  1. Open Settings → Account.
  2. Tap DANGER ZONE → DELETE ACCOUNT, then confirm.

This deletes, straight away: your player record from Cloud Firestore, your account from Firebase Authentication, and this game's Play Games cloud save (when Play Games was linked to the account). If you delete an unlinked Google account, any separate Play Games Saved Games data on this phone is preserved untouched. If any part can't be deleted, the game says so and keeps your account, so you can try again.

What happens afterwards:

  • Progress saved on your phone stays on your phone. DELETE ACCOUNT deletes your cloud account and online backups, but does not reset your device gameplay progress. All unlocked levels, stars, streaks, and shards remain intact on your device, and you transition to Guest mode.
  • The game will not sign you in again by itself on this phone. Automatic background sign-in is disabled until you explicitly tap SIGN IN (in Settings → Play Games) or WITH GOOGLE (in Settings → Account), or clear the game's data or reinstall it.

What DELETE ACCOUNT does not delete:

  • your purchase records for 30 days (section 7), and then they are deleted. Purchases can't be recovered after you delete your account; the game warns you first if you have bought something;
  • your Google Play purchases themselves (for example Remove Ads), which Google holds for your Google account;
  • your Google Account and your Play Games profile, achievements and leaderboard scores, which Google holds (delete them as described below);
  • Analytics events already sent: they aren't linked to your account, so we can't pick yours out; Google deletes them automatically after 2 months;
  • ad data held by Google, and Android's own backup of your phone;
  • emails you sent us (below).

By email, for example if you no longer have the game:

  1. Email mohitshahdadpuri3@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account", and tell us your name in the game and how you signed in (Play Games or Google). If you signed in with Google, send it from that Google account's address.
  2. We delete your account and player record within 30 days, and reply to confirm. This game's Play Games cloud save is kept by Google: delete it in the Play Games app, or with DELETE ACCOUNT in the game.

Bug reports and feedback you emailed us: email mohitshahdadpuri3@gmail.com from the address you sent them from, with the subject "Delete my data". We delete every report and feedback email from that address, with their attachments, within 30 days, and reply to confirm. We keep no copy.

Data Google holds, which you delete yourself:

  • Play Games profile and its data for this game, including the cloud save: in the Google Play Games app's settings, or at https://myaccount.google.com.
  • Older versions with ads: you can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings; version 3.0.3 no longer requests it.
  • Progress on your phone: uninstall the game, or Android Settings → Apps → KERNOS: Offline Logic Puzzles → Storage → Clear data. That doesn't delete cloud data. Android's own backup is managed in your Google account's backup settings.
